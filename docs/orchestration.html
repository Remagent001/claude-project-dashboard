Why the old playbook is done

"Agent Assist" was a suggestion box. This is a workforce.

The 2019-era pattern dropped a generic assistant beside a human and called it transformation. Custom Assistants don't sit beside the work — they do it, end to end, inside the systems you already run.

Agent Assist

the old way
  • ✕ Generic model that doesn't know your business
  • ✕ Suggests replies — a human still does everything
  • ✕ Can't touch your tools, CRM, or data
  • ✕ One bot, one trick, one window
  • ✕ Forgets the moment the chat ends

Custom Assistants

what we build
  • ✓ Trained on your context — your docs, voice, and rules
  • ✓ Completes the task, not just the suggestion
  • ✓ Calls your tools — APIs, CRM, calendars, databases
  • ✓ Many specialists, orchestrated behind one front door
  • ✓ Remembers across sessions and hand-offs
What we build

Assistants that act — wired into the work.

Every build is custom, but it's made of the same dependable parts. We assemble them around your process instead of forcing your process around a product.

🧠

Knows your business

We ground the assistant in your documents, policies, product, and tone — so answers are yours, not a generic model's best guess.

🧰

Connected to your tools

CRM, calendars, email, databases, internal APIs. The assistant reads and writes where the real work lives — securely, with permissions.

🎻

Orchestrated, not solo

A conductor routes each request to the right specialist — intake, research, drafting, review — then composes one clean result.

🗂️

Persistent memory

Context carries across sessions and hand-offs. Pick up exactly where things left off — no re-explaining every morning.

🛡️

Guardrails & review

Approvals, escalation paths, and a review step before anything ships. The assistant is bold where it's safe and cautious where it counts.

📈

Built to measure

Every run is logged and traceable, so you can see what worked, tune it, and prove the ROI to whoever signs the cheque.

How a build goes

From "we have a problem" to a working assistant.

No six-month discovery deck. We map the workflow, build the smallest thing that proves it, then orchestrate outward.

1

Map the work

We sit with the actual process — the messy parts included — and find where an assistant earns its keep.

2

Wire it up

We connect your tools and data, then stand up the first specialist assistant against a real task.

3

Orchestrate

Add specialists and a conductor to route between them — one front door, many hands behind it.

4

Hand off & tune

You run it. We watch the logs, sand the edges, and expand to the next workflow.
